02 — Profile

About Sunera

Computer Engineering student building production-grade AI systems that bridge cutting-edge research and real-world impact.

I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Ruhuna, specializing in machine learning systems that thrive beyond the lab. My work focuses on the intersection of intelligent automation, computer vision, and human-centered design.

What drives me? Deploying AI that actually works. I've architected vision-powered quality assurance systems for manufacturing floors, built adaptive learning platforms serving 80,000+ students, and engineered real-time anomaly detectors for mission-critical operations. Each project taught me that production ML is 20% models and 80% infrastructure, monitoring, and resilience engineering.

My Philosophy

Ship fast, observe closely, iterate ruthlessly. Every model needs monitoring dashboards, fallback strategies, and fairness audits before it touches users. I believe in building systems that degrade gracefully, explain their decisions, and respect user privacy.
